About

Family garden study, 2024
Color pencil, alcohol markers, pen, paper, digital art

This project is a broad exploration of cultural heritage and community love carried through plants, oral history, and resource sharing. I sought to capture the meaning and memories embedded within the garden beds and potted plants of my family's backyard. Through the slow act of live drawing, exchanging conversations, and trimming trees, I experienced the different aspects of the garden. For many of these plants, I grew up only knowing the Vietnamese names and the ways they were used in my family. This project includes a drawn diagram of my family's backyard with information on each of the plants, gathered through interviews and memories with my family.
